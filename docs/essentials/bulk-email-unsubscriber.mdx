Bulk Unsubscribe finds every newsletter and marketing list you're subscribed to, shows you which ones you actually read, and lets you unsubscribe, archive, or delete in one click.

Open **Bulk Unsubscribe** under Cleanup in the left sidebar, or visit [Bulk Unsubscribe](https://www.getinboxzero.com/bulk-unsubscribe).

## Review Senders

The **Bulk Unsubscriber** page groups newsletter and marketing email by sender, showing each sender's volume and how often you read them. Use the filters to narrow the list.

To see a sender's activity and individual messages, double-click its row or choose **View stats** from the row's **⋯** menu.

## Sender Actions

- **Unsubscribe**: Shown for senders with an unsubscribe link. When Inbox Zero can unsubscribe for you, it also archives the sender's existing emails, and a progress panel tracks the archiving. Otherwise, the button opens the sender's unsubscribe page.
- **Block**: Shown instead of Unsubscribe for senders without an unsubscribe link. Inbox Zero archives the sender's existing emails and automatically archives future ones.
- **Approve**: Keep the sender in your inbox and move it out of the unhandled list.

The row's **⋯** menu has more options:

- **Label future emails**: Apply a label to future messages from the sender. In Outlook, this is **Categorize future emails**.
- **Auto archive**: Archive the sender's existing emails and automatically archive future ones.
- **Archive all**: Archive existing messages from the sender.
- **Delete all**: Move existing messages from the sender to trash.

Use the checkboxes to apply actions to several senders at once. **Select N suggested** selects and shows the subscriptions you rarely read; while it's active, the button reads **Showing N suggested**, and clicking it again shows all senders. Review the selection before deleting, since deletion is harder to undo than archiving.
