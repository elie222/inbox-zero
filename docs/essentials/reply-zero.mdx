Reply Zero labels the conversations that need your reply and the replies you're waiting on, so nothing slips through the cracks. Conversations are labeled `To Reply` when you owe a response and `Awaiting Reply` when you're waiting on someone else. The labels (categories on Outlook) appear in your regular email client.

## Enable Reply Zero

Open **Assistant** and enable the `To Reply` and `Awaiting Reply` rules.

## Follow-Up Reminders

1. Open **Assistant** > **Settings** and turn on **Follow-up reminders**. Both thresholds start at three days.
2. Select **Configure** to choose how many days to wait in **Remind me when they haven't replied after** and **Remind me when I haven't replied after**, and whether to **Auto-generate drafts** that nudge people when you are waiting on them. Saturday and Sunday do not count toward these thresholds.
3. Select **Save**, or use **Find follow-ups** to scan existing mail immediately.

Matching conversations receive a `Follow-up` label or category. The label is removed when a new reply arrives or you send one.

To receive reminders in Slack, Microsoft Teams, or Telegram, enable follow-up delivery for the connected account on the **Channels** page. For Slack, select a direct message or an allowed private channel first. Each reminder includes **Open in Gmail** (or **Open in Outlook**) and **Mark done**, which stops reminders for that conversation.
