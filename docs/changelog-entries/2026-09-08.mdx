---
description: "Multi-Label Splits"
---

Build a split that spans several labels at once — describe what you want and AI suggests matching labels and a name, or pick them yourself. Combined results stay newest-first as you scroll.

- Reply Yes, No, or Maybe to calendar invitations right from the email reader
- Collapse the mail sidebar to an icon rail, or drag the edge to resize it
- Open Settings as a dialog from anywhere — including from Command K
- Thread list groups by date (Today, Yesterday, and earlier) so long inboxes are easier to scan
