---
description: "File Into Subfolders"
---

Auto-file can put an attachment in a folder inside a folder you already allowed. A receipt can land in Receipts/2026/Amazon instead of a new folder at the top of your drive.

- In your filing rules, say how you group things, such as by year, month, vendor, or client.
- A subfolder that already exists is reused, so the same vendor does not get a second one.
- Subfolders the assistant creates are added to your allowed folders for next time.
- A new folder at the top of your drive is created only when none of yours fits.
