description: "Copy Verification Codes"
---

A verification code in your inbox shows up as a button. Click it to copy the code, and you stay on the list.

- The notification on your phone includes the code, so you can use it without opening the email.
- Star, mark as read, or add a label, and the change shows up right away.
- Meeting briefs skip a meeting you declined, and leave out guests who declined.
