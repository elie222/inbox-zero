---
description: "Inbox Health Reports"
---

Premium accounts now get periodic Inbox Health emails that surface newsletters and senders you rarely open, with a direct path to clean them up in bulk — so you can cut inbox noise without digging through old mail.

- Emails highlight low-engagement senders and estimate how many messages you could avoid each year
- One tap takes you to bulk unsubscribe with your top suggestions already selected
- Delivered at most once a month, and you can opt out anytime in your email preferences
