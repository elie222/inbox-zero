description: "Your Week in Email"
---

Your weekly summary is back — and redesigned. Every Monday you'll get a clear recap of what was archived for you, what still needs a reply, and how many cold emails were blocked.

- Outlook threads now show your draft replies right in the conversation
- Cold Email Blocker is smarter about unsolicited outreach — specific, relevant messages get through while templated pitches stay out
- Mark emails as spam from the reader, and manage unsubscribe and auto-archive from the actions menu
- On desktop, you'll see when an update is ready so you can reload when it suits you
