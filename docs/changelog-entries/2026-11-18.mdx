---
description: "Send While It Replies"
---

You can send your next message while the assistant is still answering. It waits above the input, and it goes out on its own once the reply is saved.

- Send now stops the reply and sends right away. What the assistant already wrote stays in the chat.
- Take the waiting message off, or press stop, and it goes back into the input. Another message you send while you wait is added to the same one.
- When the assistant checks several things in a row, those steps fold into one row. It says Working... while they run. When it's done, the row says how many steps it worked through, such as Worked through 3 steps. Open it to see each step. A reply, a card, or a change to your mail stays visible, and a single step still shows on its own.
