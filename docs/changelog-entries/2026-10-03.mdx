description: "Attachment Previews"
---

Click an image or PDF attachment in a thread to open it right in Inbox Zero — no download required. Glance at the file, then download from the dialog if you need a copy.

- Works for common image formats and PDFs
- Your account timezone now drives meeting briefs, availability hours, and booking links, with a searchable city picker in Calendar settings
- On Bulk Unsubscribe, the table header stays pinned while you scroll long sender lists
