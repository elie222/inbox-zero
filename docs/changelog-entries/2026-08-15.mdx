---
description: "Unified Inbox"
---

Mail now has an All accounts view — switch between inboxes or see everything in one place. Conversations from every connected account show up together, with clear account indicators so you always know which mailbox a thread belongs to.

- Keyboard navigation keeps the focused conversation on screen, and the list loads more threads as you approach the bottom
- Unified inbox threads use compact account avatars and load labels alongside conversations
- Meeting recordings are easier to follow: see when the notetaker is waiting vs live, open processing or finished notes from Up next, and get live updates while transcripts are still generating
