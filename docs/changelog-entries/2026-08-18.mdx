---
description: "Snappier Everywhere"
---

Inbox Zero feels faster on desktop and the web. The desktop app reopens instantly and picks up where you left off, and hovering an email warms it so opening feels immediate.

- On Mac, closing the window hides the app instead of quitting, so reopening from the dock is instant
- Launch restores your last page instead of walking you through login redirects
- Hover or keyboard-focus a conversation to prefetch it before you open it
- Labels, folders, and mail settings load from a local snapshot so the sidebar appears right away
