---
description: "Translate Threads"
---

Open a foreign-language thread and hit Translate — Inbox Zero turns the messages into your language on demand. Nothing runs until you ask, so you stay in control of when AI reads the mail.

- Translate from the reader's more menu, Command Palette (Cmd+K), or Shift+T
- Each translated message shows which language it came from, with Show original to toggle back
- Messages already in your language stay put; translating again flips back without another AI call
- Chat replies resume if your connection drops mid-answer, and Stop now cancels the run for real
