---
description: "Describe Your Split"
---

Creating a split inbox is as easy as typing what you want. Describe the view — like "unread work mail" or "newsletters" — and Inbox Zero picks the matching filter and opens the new tab for you.

- Meeting Notetaker is out of early access — summaries can help draft replies when everyone on the thread was in the meeting
- Contact suggestions now work in Cc and Bcc, not just To
- The message reader shows sender photos and collapses quoted reply history
- Open any conversation in Gmail or Outlook from the thread menu
