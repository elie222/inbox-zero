description: "A Free Email App"
---

You can read and send mail in Inbox Zero for free on the web, desktop, and iPhone. Add the AI assistant only if you want it.

- When you sign up, choose the AI assistant, the email app, or both. Choosing the email app opens your inbox and skips the AI setup.
- If you only want the email app, the upgrade page lets you continue for free.
- Inbox stays in the sidebar, so you can open the app from the rest of Inbox Zero.
- Open Settings from the account menu, or press Cmd/Ctrl+, in the desktop app.
