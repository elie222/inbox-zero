---
description: "Bulk Unsubscribe Side Panel"
---

Bulk Unsubscribe now opens a side panel when you click a sender — stats, their emails, and the reader, all without leaving the page. Clear your list faster with clearer statuses and actions that stay within reach.

- Statuses are To review, All senders, Unsubscribed, Auto-archived, and Kept — and To review hides senders without an unsubscribe link unless you include them
- Turn off "Archive on unsubscribe" when you only want to stop future mail and leave existing threads alone
- Unsubscribe, Keep, and Archive sit in a floating bar at the bottom of the page
- The rarely read senders email matches the weekly summary look, with rows that link straight into Bulk Unsubscribe
