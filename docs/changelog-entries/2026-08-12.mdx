---
description: "Mail, Rebuilt"
---

The Mail screen has a new look — a cleaner three-column layout with keyboard shortcuts, split views, and threads that read as a flat message list instead of stacked cards. Opening your inbox feels faster too, thanks to a local cache that shows your mail right away while it refreshes in the background.

- Outlook users get Focused/Other inbox sections and folder navigation that matches Microsoft
- Unread subjects stand out more clearly, and thread labels stay visible across the conversation
- The Integrations page now explains what each connection does, so it's clearer what you're enabling
- Once you turn on the meeting recorder, it defaults to joining all eligible calls
