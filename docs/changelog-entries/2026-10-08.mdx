---
description: "Inline Team Comments"
---

Team comments now live in the thread itself. Open them with M (or from More actions / Command K), discuss inline under the messages, and @mention a teammate to share the conversation with them.

- Unshared threads stay quiet — comments only appear after you open them, and only if your organization has teammates
- @mentioning someone who isn't on the thread shares it with them when you send
- Reply drafts sync to your mailbox, so they show up in Gmail, Outlook, and on your other devices
- Shared with me and Activity live in your account menu, with clearer Conversations / Activity tabs on those pages
