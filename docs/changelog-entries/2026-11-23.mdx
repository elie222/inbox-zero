---
description: "Copy with Details"
---

You can copy a message, or every message in the open conversation, as plain text that includes who sent it, who it went to, and when.

- Copy one message from its menu. Copy the conversation from the thread menu, with Shift+C, or from the command palette. A conversation copy leaves drafts out.
- The text starts with the subject. Each message adds the sender, who it was sent to, the date, and any attachment names, then the message. A link keeps its address.
- Click a CSV or tab-separated attachment to open it as a table. A large file shows the first part, with a note to download the rest.
