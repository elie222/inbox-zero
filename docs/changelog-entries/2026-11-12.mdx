---
description: "Choose Your Language"
---

You can use Inbox Zero in Spanish, French, German, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Japanese, Korean, or Simplified Chinese. Choose it in Settings, under Account, or from the language menu on the public home and pricing pages.

- Your inbox, the composer, and the assistant's menus and prompts follow the language you pick.
- Rules, settings, calendars, meetings, and the tools for unsubscribing and archiving follow it too.
- Dates follow it, including Today and Yesterday. You can type a snooze date in English in any of these languages. Spanish, French, German, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese understand a date in that language as well.
- Your emails, your label names, and the assistant's replies stay in the language they were written.
