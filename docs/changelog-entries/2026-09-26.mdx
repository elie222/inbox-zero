---
description: "Pop-Out Replies"
---

Pop a reply out of the thread into its own window so you can keep browsing other mail while you write. Use the button next to "Draft to …" or press Cmd+Shift+P.

- Works for replies, forwards, and saved drafts — your text, recipients, attachments, and delivery times come with you
- Closing the window leaves the draft saved so it reappears inline; discarding from the window deletes it
- Sent replies now show up in the thread the moment you hit send
