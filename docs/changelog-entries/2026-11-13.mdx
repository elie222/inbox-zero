---
description: "Clean Up in Bulk"
---

In your inbox, ask the assistant to archive, trash, mark as read, or label every email that matches a search. A card shows an estimate and a few senders, and your mail stays as it is until you confirm.

- Describe which emails qualify, such as newsletters you never open, and it acts only on those.
- Labeling applies a label you already have, or a category you already have on Outlook.
- The cleanup runs in the background. You can watch its progress and stop it. One run covers up to 10,000 emails, and you can ask again for the rest.
- Pictures in an email open full screen, and you can move between them. When a message has more than one file, Download all saves each one.
- Snooze offers In 1 hour and In 3 hours, ahead of the choices for later days.
