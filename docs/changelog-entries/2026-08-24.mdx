---
description: "Local-First Mail"
---

Your mailbox now syncs in the background and stays available on your device, so Inbox and Unread open instantly — even when you're offline or coming back online. Archive, delete, snooze, and read/unread updates feel immediate while everything stays in sync.

- Right-click labels, categories, and folders in the sidebar to rename, recolor, or delete them
- Select all conversations in the current view with ⌘A / Ctrl+A
- All Accounts stays cache-first, so the merged inbox stays responsive while pages refresh
