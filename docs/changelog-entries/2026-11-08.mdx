---
description: "Select Emails From Chat"
---

Chat from your inbox knows the view you're in and which emails you've selected. Ask it to select some, open one, or run a search, then archive or label them with the usual buttons.

- It selects emails in the list you're looking at. Ask it to search when they aren't there, and it can select what shows up.
- Mail inside a collapsed inbox bundle stays out of that selection until you open the bundle.
- In Settings, open an email account and choose Reset Label Colors. Inbox Zero labels such as To Reply and Newsletter go back to their default colors. Labels you created are left alone.
