---
description: "Check Before You Send"
---

Before a message goes out, you'll get a heads-up if you mentioned an attachment you didn't add, or left a placeholder like [company name] in what you wrote. You can go back and fix it, or send anyway.

- The check looks at what you wrote, and leaves the quoted thread and your signature out
- Split view works in All Accounts, following the layout you already saved
- Discussions sit in the sidebar, with an unread count, when your account is in an organization
