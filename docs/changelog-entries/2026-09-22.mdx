---
description: "Weekly Summaries by Sender"
---

Your weekly summary groups cold emails and archived mail by sender, so repeat senders collapse into one row with recent subjects and a count — and you can jump straight to a message or search that sender in Gmail or Outlook.

- Each subject links to the email in your mailbox
- Sender names (and "+N more") open a search for that person's mail
- Summaries stick to weekly cold-email and archive activity, and skip the send when there's nothing new
