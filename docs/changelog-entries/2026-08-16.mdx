---
description: "Snooze"
---

You can snooze mail from Command K — pick a preset or type a natural-language time, and the conversation comes back when you want it. Messages leave your inbox right away, then return automatically at the time you chose.

- Open a sender profile from the mail reader to see public professional context about who you're talking to
- Collapse the mail sidebar when you want more room for your list and reader
- Try Inbox Zero as a desktop app, with sign-in through your system browser
