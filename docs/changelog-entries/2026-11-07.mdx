---
description: "Add Senders to a Split"
---

Select a few emails and add their senders to a split. Mail from those people shows up there, so you can group them without typing each address.

- Cmd-click or Ctrl-click selects an email without opening it. Once one is selected, a plain click adds or removes another. Shift-click still selects a range, and Enter still opens.
- Add senders to split is in the More menu on the selection bar and in the command palette. Senders the split already covers are skipped.
- A split can hold 25 conditions, up from 10.
- Process Past Emails keeps going when your email provider slows you down. It pauses, tells you when it will continue, and picks up where it left off. On a trial, one run can cover up to 2,000 past emails.
