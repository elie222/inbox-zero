You can download Inbox Zero for Mac and Windows from the new desktop page. The app keeps itself up to date, and Mac builds are signed and notarized so they open without Gatekeeper friction.

- Meeting follow-up drafts open in Gmail or Outlook instead of Inbox Zero Mail
- Press H to snooze the selected conversation from the keyboard
- Thread rows hide Gmail's system category labels so your own labels stay front and center
- Combined inbox selection and caching feel closer to working in a single account
