description: "Split Inbox Library"
---

Split inboxes go beyond multi-label filters. Combine labels, categories, senders, unread, starred, and message age with match-all or match-any logic — or pick a preset from the new library and edit the split in place.

- Ready-made General and Apps presets (including GitHub and Stripe) so common splits are one click away
- AI still suggests a split from plain language, and you can edit those conditions before saving
- Conversation list is easier to scan — wider sender names, plus "Last 7 days" and "Earlier this month" instead of a heading for every day
