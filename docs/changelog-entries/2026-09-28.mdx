description: "Connect Popular Apps"
---

HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, and other apps you already use now connect through their own sign-in — no Pipedream account needed first. Once connected, your assistant can look things up in those apps while you draft and triage.

- Eleven apps are ready out of the box: HubSpot, Salesforce, Airtable, Google Sheets, Slack, Asana, ClickUp, Jira, Zendesk, QuickBooks, and Calendly
- Each connection uses that app's own OAuth screen
- Tools are curated and read-only so the assistant can look things up without changing your data
- The Integrations list is cleaner, with just the app names
