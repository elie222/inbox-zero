---
description: "Richer Compose"
---

Writing email in Inbox Zero feels closer to a real mail client. Compose opens as a quiet panel with formatting, links, lists, attachments, and inline images — while quoted replies and your signature stay protected.

- Choose which connected account to send from when composing in All Accounts
- Expand the composer to fullscreen when you need more room, or keep it compact
- Paste rich text and insert links without fighting the editor
- Unsupported layouts fall back safely so complex messages aren't mangled
