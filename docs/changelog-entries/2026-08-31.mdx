---
description: "Search Your Mail"
---

Find anything in your mailbox without leaving Inbox Zero. There's now a real search box on the mail screen — type a query, hit Enter, and results come straight from Gmail or Outlook.

- Use Gmail operators like `from:` and `has:attachment` when searching a Google account
- Outlook search returns relevance-ranked matches across your mailbox
- Clear a search with Escape or the X button; the query stays in the URL so you can share or refresh the view
