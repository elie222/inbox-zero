---
description: "OTP Alerts on Mobile"
---

When a verification code or OTP email lands in your inbox, the Inbox Zero mobile app can now push it straight to your phone — so you don't have to dig through email while logging into another app or site.

- Push notifications fire as soon as a new OTP or verification-code email arrives
- Inbox sync on mobile is faster and more reliable with incremental updates instead of full reloads
