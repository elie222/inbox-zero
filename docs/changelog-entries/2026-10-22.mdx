---
description: "Mail and Assistant"
---

Mail and Assistant sit together at the top of either sidebar, so you can move between your inbox and the assistant from the same place. On a phone, open navigation from the mail toolbar.

- Print a message or the whole conversation. Download a message, or open the original source to copy or save it.
- From the conversation menu, start a rule already filled in for the latest person who wrote to you.
- An empty inbox shows a sunrise and says You've reached inbox zero. A cleared split says All clear.
- Settings is at the bottom of the mail sidebar, just above your account.
