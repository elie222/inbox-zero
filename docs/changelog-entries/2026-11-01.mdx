---
description: "Pick a Signature"
---

You can save more than one signature and choose which one to use while you write. That covers a different close for each language, or for work and everything else.

- Under More signatures in Settings, add, edit, or delete a named signature.
- Find in sent mail looks through recent messages and offers signatures you already use, so you can save one without typing it again.
- Once you have more than one, a signature button in the composer swaps the one in the draft. The Sent with line stays.
- Drag an account in the account switcher to put your accounts in the order you want. The order is saved, and the number shortcuts follow it.
