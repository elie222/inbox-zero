---
description: "Reply from the Right Address"
---

When someone writes to a Gmail address you send as, your reply now goes out from that address instead of your default one.

- A From picker appears when you can send from more than one address, including on a new message
- Switching the address brings in that address's Gmail signature and leaves what you wrote alone
- AI drafts and auto-replies use the matching address too
- After you send a new message, the confirmation offers View so you can open it without leaving your place
