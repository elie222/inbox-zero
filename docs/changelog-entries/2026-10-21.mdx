---
description: "Reply All"
---

Hover a message that went to other people, and Reply all shows up next to Reply and Forward. It sends to the sender and copies the other recipients, leaving you off the list.

- Drag a split tab to reorder your inboxes. The new order stays after you reload.
- A URL at the end of a message goes out as a link, and bare URLs and email addresses in emails you open are clickable too.
- In an open conversation, Cmd/Ctrl+A selects every message so you can copy the thread as text.
- Select a passage and press Enter to reply with that text quoted.
- To Reply notifications on your phone include a short preview of the email.
