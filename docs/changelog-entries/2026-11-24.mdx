---
description: "Group by Sender"
---

Several emails from the same sender now sit on one row in your inbox, so a long run of them is easier to scan.

- It's on for every account. Turn it off from View options in the inbox, next to Bundle low-priority emails. It works on its own, on every inbox tab. One conversation stays as its own row.
- A conversation you replied to, sent, or drafted stays on its own row. So do starred mail and a sign-in code. Mail in a split you left out of bundling, such as To Reply, stays ungrouped on your other inbox tabs, and groups by sender on that split's own tab.
- The row names the sender and shows how many conversations it holds. You see the newest subject, with the older ones listed in the preview. Open the row to see each conversation. Archive or mark read on that row applies to every conversation in it.
- Search, the inbox for all your accounts, Sent, Archive, and a label or folder from the sidebar still list each conversation on its own.
- In the Mac desktop app, back and forward arrows sit beside the sidebar toggle. ⌘[ and ⌘] move through emails you opened.
