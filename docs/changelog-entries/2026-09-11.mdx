---
description: "Important and Other"
---

Your Gmail inbox can now surface Important mail in its own split, and anything that doesn't match your other splits lands in Other — so filtered tabs stay focused without hiding the rest of your inbox.

- Turn on Important to browse what Gmail marks as priority, alongside the splits you already built
- Other catches inbox conversations that match none of your enabled splits
- Disabling a split moves those conversations back into Other; All stays available for the full unfiltered view
