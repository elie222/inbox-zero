description: "Domain Filters for Splits"
---

Build a split for everyone at a company in one step — sender conditions now match an entire domain like `@acme.com`, across Gmail and Outlook.

- Assistant History groups activity by conversation, with expandable message-level details as you dig in
- When you process past email in bulk, draft replies stay off unless you turn them on
- New messages get the same compose experience as replies, including Send later and Remind me
- Drag your split tabs into the order you want from Your splits
