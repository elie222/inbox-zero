---
description: "Send Later"
---

Need a minute before you hit send? You can now schedule replies for later and set Remind me so follow-ups don't slip — right from the thread you're answering.

- Draft replies stick as you navigate and reload, including recipients, attachments, and formatting
- Offline or failed sends stay recoverable so you can retry when you're back online
- Enter opens a reply, arrow keys move between messages, and the toolbar expands or collapses the thread
- Cleaner reader and composer: dates on the right, editable recipients and subject, formatting tools when you select text
