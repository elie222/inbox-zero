---
description: "Undo Send"
---

Hit send by mistake? Your message is held for 30 seconds so you can undo — even if you close the tab or quit the app. Undo from the toast and your draft comes right back.

- Closing the tab or quitting the app no longer ends the undo window early
- After you send a reply, the thread jumps to your sent message so you can keep reading
- Popular apps like HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, and Airtable now show up as their own rows on Integrations
