---
description: "Bundle Low-Priority Mail"
---

Newsletters, notifications, and other low-priority mail can sit on one row in your inbox. The conversations you need stay easy to scan, and new mail still comes to the top.

- Turn it on from the inbox toolbar. The first time, splits you already have for notifications, newsletters, marketing, receipts, calendar, and cold email are included.
- Right-click a split and choose Bundle in Inbox to change which ones collapse.
- Open a bundle to see the conversations inside. Archive or mark read on the bundle applies to every conversation in it, and you can undo.
- Starred mail, sign-in codes, and anything that also matches a split you left out, such as To Reply, stay on their own row. So does a single conversation.
- In mail, Sign out is in the account switcher, under Add or manage accounts.
