---
description: "Open and Send Right Away"
---

Opening an email and sending a reply happen right away. The text is on screen as soon as you open a message, and the composer closes as soon as you send.

- Schedule a reply and the composer closes at once. The conversation shows Scheduling for… until it's confirmed.
- If scheduling doesn't go through, the composer comes back with your reply and what went wrong.
- An email with images shows the text first. The images fill in after.
- A long conversation, and a message that just arrived, open much sooner.
