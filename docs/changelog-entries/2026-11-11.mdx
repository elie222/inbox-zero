---
description: "Splits From Chat"
---

Ask the assistant to add or change a split inbox. When you're looking at that account's mail, the split opens so you can see what it catches.

- In the split builder, Sent to matches mail addressed to an address or a domain, in To or Cc.
- Asking to remove a split shows a card first. Delete split takes the tab away and leaves your mail as it is.
- Paste Markdown into an email you're writing and it comes through formatted. Hold Shift while you paste to keep it as typed.
- When you're in your inbox, an email the assistant shows opens over the list. Step through them there, or choose Open in reader.
