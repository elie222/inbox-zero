---
description: "Richer Compose Editing"
---

The composer now edits real email HTML, so signatures with tables, styled blocks, and hosted logos stay intact when you write and send — instead of getting flattened or locked as read-only blocks.

- Recipient suggestions pull from mail already on your device, even without Google Contacts access
- Slack confirmation cards show your full pending draft (not a truncated preview), with Send on the last card for very long messages
- Assistant replies follow the language of the email you're answering, not the language you're chatting in — unless you ask otherwise
- After snooze, trash, spam, or move, the reader advances to the next conversation the way archive already does
