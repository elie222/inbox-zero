---
description: "Set Up Your Inbox"
---

A card at the bottom of your inbox walks you through a list or split view, the tabs you want, which tab opens first, and whether newsletters and notifications sit on one row. On Gmail you also choose what All includes, and every choice saves right away so the inbox updates as you go.

- New accounts see the card the first time they open the inbox. If you already have an account, open View options in the toolbar and choose Set up your inbox. That icon pulses until you finish.
- Turn on tabs from your labels, such as To Reply and Newsletter, plus Unread. On Gmail you can add Starred, and leave tabs out of All.
- Your inbox opens on the tab you pick. If that tab is removed later, it opens on the first one.
- Close the card when you're done. Open it again from View options, where it says Run mail setup again, or from Mail setup in the command palette.
