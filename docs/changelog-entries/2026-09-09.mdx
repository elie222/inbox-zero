---
description: "Sign In With a Code"
---

Need someone to help with your inbox without sharing your Google or Microsoft password? Turn on email code sign-in in Settings, then they can sign in with a one-time code sent to your email.

- Opt in from Settings — it's off by default, and turning it off immediately revokes any active code sessions
- Codes are single-use, expire in minutes, and are rate-limited so they can't be spammed
- Code-based sessions stay restricted: they can't change the setting, pull provider credentials, or create unrestricted mobile sessions
