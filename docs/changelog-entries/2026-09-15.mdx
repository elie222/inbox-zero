---
description: "Advanced Search"
---

Find mail the way you mean to — open Search options from the search bar to filter by From, To, Subject, keywords, size, date, folder or label, and attachments, then search with a Gmail-style query you can still edit.

- Undo send for a few seconds after you hit Send — cancel delivery and get your draft back
- See when your sent messages are opened, with Opened or Not opened in Sent and a Read status toggle per account in Settings
- Open more than one mailbox window on the desktop app, and pick up where you left off when you relaunch
