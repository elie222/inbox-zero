---
description: "Choose Your Accounts"
---

Running several inboxes? You can now pick which connected accounts show up in All Accounts — so your combined mailbox only includes the ones you care about.

- Open the account picker from All Accounts and save your selection; it sticks across sessions
- New inboxes get ready-made splits for To Reply, Newsletters, Marketing, Calendar, Receipts, and more
- On desktop, Inbox Zero opens to Mail by default so you're in your inbox faster
