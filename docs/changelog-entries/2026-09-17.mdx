---
description: "Instant Local Search"
---

Mail search starts answering as you type — matches from mail already on your device show up right away, then update when the full provider search finishes, including when you're offline.

- The sidebar keeps Inbox up front and tucks Drafts, Sent, Archived, Starred, Scheduled, Spam, and Trash into a collapsible Mail group, with those views available when you need them
- Gmail label list and message-list visibility settings are respected, and you can change them from a label's right-click menu
- New messages you start composing save to your mailbox Drafts, so they stay available in Gmail or Outlook after you close the composer
