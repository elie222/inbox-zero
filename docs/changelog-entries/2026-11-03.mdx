---
description: "Chat About This Email"
---

A chat button on an open email starts the assistant with the latest message attached. Ask about it without copying the text in.

- The email sits in the composer as a card, with the subject and who sent it. Remove it when you want to ask about something else.
- Open another email and the attached message changes with it. Close the email and it goes away.
- Unsubscribe is on the reader toolbar when the sender includes a way to unsubscribe.
