Inbox Zero is your AI email assistant. It organizes your inbox, drafts replies in your voice, and helps you reach inbox zero fast. It works with both Gmail and Outlook.

When you connect your inbox, Inbox Zero sets you up with a smart set of default rules: newsletters, marketing, and notifications are filed out of the way, and emails that need an answer get a draft reply written in your voice, waiting in your drafts folder. From there, you can tell your assistant how to handle your email in plain English, and it takes care of the rest.
